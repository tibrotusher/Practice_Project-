Crop Details

This section offers detailed information on a variety of crops,
covering everything from ideal growing conditions to post-harvest handling.

Grains Farming

Plant in loose, well-drained soil to allow roots to grow freely.
Keep soil moist but not waterlogged. Thin seedlings to avoid overcrowding.

Fruits Farming

Grow in full sunlight with well-drained soil.
Stake or cage plants to support growth and prevent disease. ex.Use mulch to retain moisture and control weeds.

Vegetables Farming

Plant in well-drained, slightly acidic soil.
Space trees well apart for good air circulation to prevent fungal diseases. Prune regularly to remove dead wood and encourage new growth.
