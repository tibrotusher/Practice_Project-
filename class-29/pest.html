Pest Control Strategies

Our integrated pest management section is dedicated to providing sustainable and effective solutions for pest control.

Chemical Control

Healthy Soil: Healthy plants are less susceptible to pests,
so improve soil with compost and organic matter. Diverse Planting: Plant a variety of crops to confuse pests and reduce the spread.

Biological Control

Neem Oil: Effective against aphids, spider mites, and other insects.
Spray it on leaves and stems. Diatomaceous Earth: Sprinkle around plants to deter slugs, snails, and crawling insects.

Cultural Control

Ladybugs: Great for controlling aphids and other small pests.
Predatory Nematodes: Kill soil-dwelling pests like grubs and root weevils. Lacewing Larvae: Eat aphids, mealybugs, and other small pests.

Pest Monitoring

Use sticky traps, visual inspections, and record-keeping to detect pests early. Take action before infestations spread.

Early Detection

Conduct regular crop checks and use pheromone traps to identify pests before they cause major damage.

Effective Treatment

Apply biopesticides or eco-friendly treatments precisely when and where needed to manage pests sustainably.
